Opening paragraph. Lead with the finding or the central argument, then the
reasoning. Aim for a hook in the first three sentences that passes the
Attribution Test.<Note id="1" />

## A section heading

Body copy. Use specific numbers, named tools, real anecdotes. Anonymize names
when needed but keep the texture concrete.

<PullQuote>
  The strongest line of the piece, pulled out and set in display serif.
</PullQuote>

## Another section

More body. Use `<Callout>` for asides:

<Callout variant="note" title="Heads up">
  This is the note variant. There's also `aside` and `warn`.
</Callout>

Reference an external resource for the citation block at the bottom.<Note id="2" />

## Closing

End with a deliberate landing, not a taper. The last sentence should be one
the reader could screenshot.
